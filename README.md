# NiftyCarts — Rustic Carts & Wagons

NiftyCarts 9minecraft Mod brings wooden carts and wagons into your world for hauling goods and farming land. You can hitch these physical vehicles to yourself or pull them with horses to move items between builds. The mod also includes helpful farming tools to plow, seed, and harvest crops in open fields.

Adds seven different draft carts and wagons as physical vehicles you can pull. Lets you craft carts in every wood type to match your building style.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **jmb05**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
